# AR Specialist – Medical Billing

## Overview

Accounts Receivable (AR) management is a key part of the Revenue Cycle Management process. The AR Specialist is responsible for monitoring outstanding claims, following up with insurance companies, identifying payment issues, resolving denials, and ensuring timely reimbursement.

## AR Workflow

1. Review outstanding accounts
2. Analyze AR aging
3. Check claim status
4. Contact insurance payer
5. Identify denial or rejection reason
6. Take corrective action
7. Resubmit or appeal the claim when required
8. Document follow-up activity
9. Monitor payment
10. Close the account after resolution

## AR Aging Categories

| Aging Category | Description |
|---|---|
| 0–30 Days | Recently submitted claims |
| 31–60 Days | Claims requiring monitoring |
| 61–90 Days | Claims requiring active follow-up |
| 91–120 Days | High-priority AR |
| 120+ Days | Critical/aged AR requiring escalation |

## Key AR Activities

- Insurance claim follow-up
- Claim status verification
- Denial investigation
- Rejection correction
- Payment variance analysis
- Underpayment identification
- Appeal follow-up
- Patient balance review
- AR aging analysis
- Documentation of collection activity

## Important AR KPIs

- Total AR
- AR over 90 days
- Days in AR
- Collection rate
- Denial rate
- Claim resolution rate
- Average follow-up time
- Payment turnaround time

## Objective

The objective of AR management is to reduce outstanding receivables, resolve unpaid claims, minimize aging AR, improve collections, and maintain an efficient revenue cycle.
