Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) involves multiple processes that work together to support accurate billing, timely reimbursement, and effective accounts receivable management.

## KPI Analysis

RCM performance can be evaluated by comparing operational results against targets and identifying areas requiring improvement.

### Clean Claim Rate

Clean Claim Rate measures the percentage of claims accepted for processing without requiring correction.

Clean Claim Rate = Clean Claims ÷ Total Claims × 100

### Denial Rate

Denial Rate measures the percentage of submitted claims that are denied by the payer.

Denial Rate = Denied Claims ÷ Total Claims × 100

### Collection Rate

Collection Rate measures the percentage of collectible revenue that has been successfully collected.

Collection Rate = Collections ÷ Collectible Amount × 100

### AR Over 90 Days

AR over 90 days represents outstanding accounts that have remained unresolved for more than 90 days.

Higher aged AR may indicate delayed claim resolution, denials, payer issues, or follow-up gaps.

## Root-Cause Analysis

The RCM analysis should identify the underlying cause of recurring problems.

Example:

Recurring Eligibility Denials  
      ↓  
Review Denial Data  
      ↓  
Identify Pattern  
      ↓  
Eligibility Not Verified  
      ↓  
Process Gap Identified  
      ↓  
Improve Eligibility Verification  
      ↓  
Monitor Denial Rate

The same approach can be applied to authorization, coding, timely filing, provider enrollment, and documentation issues.

## Corrective and Preventive Action

Corrective action addresses the current issue.

Preventive action addresses the underlying process to reduce the possibility of recurrence.

Example:

Problem: High authorization denial rate

Corrective Action: Review denied claims and submit appropriate corrections or appeals.

Preventive Action: Add authorization verification before claim submission.
